About Previa

Previa is an international property marketplace. We list homes, commercial space and land for sale and rent across eight European markets, and we build the tools brokers and private sellers use to reach buyers.

Property search is still fragmented by country. A buyer looking at Tallinn and Helsinki works across two portals with different filters, different conventions and no shared map. Previa exists to remove that friction: one search, one map, one account, across every market we cover.

We are a marketplace, not an agency. We do not take commission on a sale and we do not compete with the brokers who list here. Sellers pay for a listing package; what they agree with a broker is between them.

Every broker profile shows credentials, languages, active listings and completed transactions, because who you deal with matters as much as what you are buying.

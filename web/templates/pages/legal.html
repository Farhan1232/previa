1. What we collect

Account details you provide, listings you publish, searches you save, and standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

2. How we use it

To operate your account, show relevant listings, send the alerts you ask for, process package payments, and prevent fraudulent or abusive use of the platform.

3. Cookies

Previa sets cookies for sign-in, your selected country, your theme preference and aggregate analytics. You can change your choices at any time on the cookie preferences page.

4. Sharing with brokers

When you send an enquiry, the details you enter are passed to the broker or seller for that listing so they can reply. We do not sell personal data.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to processing or request portability.

6. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is open. Expired listings are retained for statistical purposes and removed on request.

7. Contact

Data protection enquiries go to privacy@previa.estate.
